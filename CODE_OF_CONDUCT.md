# Code of conduct

MousePods follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be kind and patient. People here come from very different backgrounds, and many are new to
  open source or write in a second language.
- Talk about the code and the hardware, not about the person.
- No harassment, insults, or sexual, discriminatory or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Don't share other people's private information (including device identifiers from their captures).

If someone breaks these rules, or makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer
[@starman-tech](https://github.com/starman-tech) through GitHub. Reports are handled privately.
The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions, and ban people who keep breaking
these rules.
